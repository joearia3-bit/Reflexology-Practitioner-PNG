MY STORY

Welcome. I'm Joseph Aria.

I am a Reflexology Practitioner based in Papua New Guinea. My journey into Reflexology came through personal experience, searching, learning and discovering practical ways to support my own wellbeing.

Over the years, Reflexology became more than something I practiced for myself. It became a way of serving and supporting other people through compassionate, one-on-one wellness sessions.

Today, I continue to develop my practice with a focus on listening carefully to each person, understanding their needs and providing a calm and respectful wellness experience.
