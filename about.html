MY STORY

Welcome. I'm Joseph Aria.

My name is Joseph Aria. I am a Reflexology Practitioner from Papua New Guinea.

I am from Barakau Village, Central Province, Papua New Guinea. I am the second-born son of Pastor Aria Hegame and Linda Loa Mada.

My journey into Reflexology came through a major turning point in my life. In 2013, I experienced a brain haemorrhagic stroke that significantly changed my life.

I had to learn new ways to live, move and work. During this difficult period, I began searching for information about natural approaches to wellness and recovery.

This search eventually introduced me to Reflexology. I began studying and practising Reflexology on myself and others.

Over time, Reflexology became an important part of my personal journey and my desire to serve and support other people through complementary wellness.

MY REFLEXOLOGY JOURNEY

From Personal Experience to Service

My own experience encouraged me to continue learning and developing my practical knowledge of Reflexology.

Through consistent practice, determination and a positive approach, I worked on improving my balance, movement, strength and motor skills.

Today, I continue to develop my Reflexology practice, with a particular interest in traditional Chinese Reflexology techniques and holistic approaches to wellness.

My goal is simple: to provide a calm, respectful and compassionate environment where people can take time for themselves and their wellbeing.

CHINESE REFLEXOLOGY

Reflexology & Holistic Wellness

My practice is influenced by traditional Chinese Reflexology techniques and holistic approaches to wellness.

I believe wellness involves looking at the person as a whole and creating space for relaxation, body awareness and personal wellbeing.

Reflexology is offered as a complementary wellness practice and is approached according to each person's comfort and individual needs.

Important Wellness Information

Reflexology is provided as a complementary wellness practice. It is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure medical conditions.

Reflexology should not replace professional medical advice, prescribed medication or other medical care.

If you have a medical concern, serious symptoms or an emergency, please seek advice or care from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
