About Reflexology Practitioner PNG

A personal journey of recovery, restoration, wellness and service through Reflexology.

About Joseph Aria

My name is Joseph Aria, from Barakau Village, Central Province, Papua New Guinea. I am the second-born son of Pastor Aria Hegame and Linda Loa Mada.

My journey into Reflexology began after a major turning point in my life. In 2013, I experienced a brain haemorrhagic stroke that changed almost every part of my life.

The stroke affected the left side of my body significantly. I had to learn many everyday movements again and find new ways to live, work and support myself.

During this difficult period, I began searching for information about natural approaches to wellness and recovery. This search eventually introduced me to Reflexology.

My Reflexology Journey

Reflexology became an important part of my personal journey. Through consistent practice, learning and determination, I worked on improving my balance, movement, strength and motor skills.

My experience encouraged me to continue studying and practising Reflexology, not only for myself but also to support other people seeking complementary wellness support.

Over the years, I have continued developing my knowledge and practical experience, with a particular interest in Chinese Reflexology and holistic wellness.

My Approach

At Reflexology Practitioner PNG, my approach is centred on listening, compassion, care and respect for every individual.

Each person is different, so I take time to understand the person's needs and provide a personalised Reflexology wellness experience.

My practice is influenced by Chinese Reflexology, holistic wellness principles and my own years of personal and practical experience.

Important Wellness Notice

Reflexology is provided as a complementary wellness practice. It is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure medical conditions and should not replace professional medical advice, prescribed medication or other medical care.

If you have a medical concern, please consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

MY STORY

Welcome. I'm Joseph Aria.

I am a Reflexology Practitioner based in Papua New Guinea. My journey into Reflexology came through personal experience, searching, learning and discovering practical ways to support my own wellbeing.

Over the years, Reflexology became more than something I practiced for myself. It became a way of serving and supporting other people through compassionate, one-on-one wellness sessions.

Today, I continue to develop my practice with a focus on listening carefully to each person, understanding their needs and providing a calm and respectful wellness experience.
