ABOUT JOSEPH

Meet Joseph Aria

My name is Joseph Aria, and I am a Reflexology Practitioner from Papua New Guinea.

I am from Barakau Village, Central Province, Papua New Guinea, and I am the second-born son of Pastor Aria Hegame and Linda Loa Mada.

My journey into reflexology developed from my own search for recovery, restoration and improved wellbeing following a major turning point in my life.

Through years of study, practice and personal experience, reflexology became an important part of my wellness journey.

Today, I am committed to sharing what I have learned with others through respectful, compassionate and personalised reflexology sessions.

MY JOURNEY

From Struggle to Restoration

My journey has included seasons of difficulty, uncertainty and significant personal challenges.

Following a brain haemorrhagic stroke in 2013, my life changed dramatically. I had to relearn many things and find new ways to live and work.

My experience taught me the importance of perseverance, patience, positive thinking, compassion and hope.

During my search for ways to support my own recovery and wellbeing, I discovered reflexology.

I began studying and practising reflexology, applying what I learned to myself and later to family members, friends and other people.

Over the years, reflexology has become more than a practice for me. It has become an important part of my personal journey and my desire to serve others.

WELLNESS

Reflexology & Your Wellness

Reflexology is a complementary wellness practice involving pressure techniques applied to areas such as the feet.

My purpose is to provide a supportive environment that encourages relaxation and general wellbeing.

Every person's experience is different, and reflexology sessions are approached according to the individual's comfort and needs.

Important:

Reflexology is offered as complementary wellness support. It is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure medical conditions, and it is not a replacement for professional medical care or prescribed medication.
