ABOUT

Welcome to Joseph Aria Reflexology

I am Joseph Aria, a Reflexology Practitioner from Papua New Guinea. My journey into reflexology began through my own search for recovery, restoration and better wellness.

Through years of learning and personal practice, reflexology became an important part of my wellness journey.

Today, I use my experience to support others through compassionate, respectful and personalised reflexology sessions.

MY JOURNEY

From Struggle to Restoration

My journey was not an easy one. After experiencing a major turning point in my life, I had to learn how to rebuild my strength, confidence and independence.

Reflexology became part of that journey. I studied, practised and continued learning while applying what I learned to myself and others.

My experience has given me a deep appreciation for patience, compassion, perseverance and hope.

UNDERSTANDING REFLEXOLOGY

Why Reflexology?

Reflexology is a complementary wellness practice involving specific pressure and massage techniques.

People may seek Reflexology for:

  • Relaxation
  • General wellbeing
  • Stress support
  • Body awareness
  • A sense of balance
  • Complementary wellness support
Important:

Reflexology is not a replacement for professional medical diagnosis, emergency care, prescribed medication or medical treatment.
