MY JOURNEY

From Struggle to Restoration

In 2013, I experienced a brain haemorrhagic stroke that dramatically changed my life.

I lost my job and had to learn new ways to live, work and move forward.

My journey taught me patience, perseverance, positive thinking, compassion and hope.

During my search for ways to support my own wellness, I discovered Reflexology.

I began studying and practising Reflexology on myself and others. Over time, it became an important part of my personal journey and my desire to serve others.

WELLNESS

Reflexology & Your Wellness

Reflexology is a complementary wellness practice involving pressure techniques applied to areas such as the feet.

My purpose is to provide supportive Reflexology sessions that encourage relaxation and general wellbeing.

Every person's experience is different. Sessions are therefore approached according to the individual's comfort and needs.

Important

Reflexology is offered as complementary wellness support. It is not intended to diagnose, treat or cure medical conditions, and it is not a replacement for professional medical care or prescribed medication.
