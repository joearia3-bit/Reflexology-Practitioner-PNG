MY JOURNEY

From Brokenness to Recovery and Restoration

A personal journey that changed the way I understand resilience, wellness, service and hope.

MY STORY

A Journey I Never Expected

My journey into Reflexology was not something I originally planned. It grew from a period of tremendous personal change and difficulty.

What began as a search for ways to support my own recovery eventually became a practice through which I could support other people in their own wellness journeys.

These experiences shaped my understanding of patience, perseverance, compassion and the importance of never giving up hope.

THE JOURNEY

From Struggle to Service

Each stage of the journey taught me something that continues to influence my work today.

01
BEFORE THE STROKE

Life Before Everything Changed

Before my health crisis, I was working, building relationships and looking toward the future. Life had its challenges, but I was moving forward with plans and hope.

02
2013 — THE STROKE

A Life-Changing Moment

In 2013, I experienced a brain haemorrhagic stroke. My life changed dramatically, and I had to begin learning how to do many things again.

The experience affected my movement, independence, work and everyday life.

03
THE STRUGGLE

Learning to Live Differently

After the stroke, many things that once seemed simple became difficult.

I experienced significant changes in employment, finances, relationships, independence and daily life.

It was a difficult season, but it also became a season in which I learned perseverance and the importance of hope.

04
SEARCHING FOR ANSWERS

Looking for a Way Forward

I began searching widely for information that could help me understand my body and support my recovery.

I explored natural approaches, body awareness and different wellness practices.

My search eventually led me to Reflexology.

05
DISCOVERING REFLEXOLOGY

Finding a Practice That Changed My Direction

Reflexology became an important part of my personal wellness journey.

I studied Reflexology techniques, practiced consistently and paid close attention to how my body responded.

Over time, the practice became an important part of my daily discipline and personal development.

06
PRACTICE & RECOVERY

Relearning, Practicing and Growing

Recovery required patience. I had to relearn balance, movement and the use of my affected side.

I practiced Reflexology on myself while continuing to learn about the relationship between the feet, body awareness, relaxation and wellbeing.

The journey taught me that progress can happen one step at a time.

07
HELPING OTHERS

Turning Personal Experience Into Service

As my experience with Reflexology grew, I began practicing with other people.

I learned to listen carefully, understand each person's needs and provide sessions with patience, compassion and respect.

This helped shape the way I practice Reflexology today.

08
TODAY

Serving Through Reflexology

Today, I continue my journey as a Reflexology Practitioner in Papua New Guinea.

My goal is to provide a professional, compassionate and respectful wellness experience for every person I serve.

My journey continues — with gratitude, hope and a commitment to supporting wellness one step at a time.

WHAT THE JOURNEY TAUGHT ME

Lessons I Carry Into My Practice

01

Patience

Meaningful progress can take time. Every person's journey is different.

02

Perseverance

Difficult seasons can teach us to continue moving forward one step at a time.

03

Compassion

Personal experience has taught me the importance of listening and caring.

04

Hope

Hope can provide strength during difficult seasons and encourage us to keep moving forward.

MY PHILOSOPHY

LISTEN. CARE. SERVE. SUPPORT.

My experiences have shaped the way I approach every person I work with. I believe people deserve to be heard, respected and treated with compassion.
