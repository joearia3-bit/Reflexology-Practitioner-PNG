UNDERSTANDING REFLEXOLOGY

What Is Reflexology?

Reflexology is a complementary wellness practice that uses specific pressure and massage techniques on reflex areas of the feet and sometimes the hands.

A Reflexology session provides dedicated time to slow down, relax and become more aware of the body.

My approach focuses on creating a calm, respectful and personalised wellness experience for each person.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Reflexology Is Complementary Wellness Support

Reflexology is intended as a complementary wellness practice supporting relaxation, wellbeing and body awareness.

It does not replace professional medical diagnosis, emergency care, prescribed medication or medical treatment.

If you have a medical condition, serious symptoms or a health concern, please seek advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.
